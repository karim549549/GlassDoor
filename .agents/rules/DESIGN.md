# Devs Arena Design System & Aesthetic Architecture

## Visual Foundation
- **Theme**: Premium High-Tech Dark Cybernetic Stage (`#070a0d`) with Brutalist Structural Precision.
- **Accents**: 
  - Vivid Safety Orange (`#ff6b00` / `var(--orange)`) for CTAs, active ratings, and sprint states.
  - Electric Blue / Cyan (`#38bdf8`) for tamper-evident proof seals and cryptographic hashes.
  - Emerald Green (`#10b981`) for verified rubrics, pass states, and sealed credentials.
  - Ice White (`#f2f5f9`) for high-contrast legible headings and body copy.
  - Hairline Borders (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`) for precise technical cards.

## Typography
- **Headings**: Heavy geometric sans/display with tight tracking (`-0.035em`), uppercase precision, and high visual hierarchy.
- **Data & Telemetry**: Monospace (`Geist Mono`, `Space Mono`, `Courier New`) with uppercase tabular numbers, coordinate timestamps, and badge tags.
- **Body**: Clean legible sans with balanced whitespace and high contrast.

## Interactive Micro-Interactions
- **3D Particles / WebGL Field**: Responsive canvas background with particle nodes reacting smoothly to mouse movement.
- **HUD Reticles**: Subtle corner ticks and crosshair badges on key cards.
- **Magnetic Buttons & Cards**: Smooth CSS transitions on hover with inset highlights.
- **Storytelling Timeline**: Step-by-step interactive flow showing how competition produces cryptographic hiring credentials.
