# Design Engineering & Modern Aesthetic Standards

## Aesthetic & Visual Language

1. **High-Contrast Dark Mode & Precision Telemetry**:
   - Palette: Deep stage black (`#070a0d`, `#0b0f14`), crisp ice-white typography (`#f2f5f9`), warm safety orange accents (`#ff6b00` / `var(--orange)`), cyan telemetry pings (`#38bdf8`), and hairline dividers (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`).
   - Typography: Monospace telemetry readouts (Geist Mono / Courier) paired with geometric display headings with tight tracking (`letter-spacing: -0.035em`).

2. **HUD Elements & Engineering Precision**:
   - Corner bracket reticles (`c-tl`, `c-tr`, `c-bl`, `c-br`), coordinate crosshairs, live ticker readouts, pulsing status dots (`[✓] ACTIVE SPRINT`), and proof credential verification badges.
   - Interactive hover cards with subtle border glows and inset box-shadows.

3. **Three-Sided Business Storytelling**:
   The homepage must speak with intense clarity and emotional resonance to all 3 core personas:
   - **Persona 1: The Developer / Contestant**
     - Value Prop: "Stop sending CVs into the black hole. Compete in live sprint arenas, build verifiable production systems, and earn an unforgeable Proof Packet credential that lets Egyptian top companies hire you instantly."
   - **Persona 2: The Recruiter / Hiring Partner**
     - Value Prop: "Zero resume fluff. Browse pre-vetted engineers ranked by real Glicko-2 ratings across Backend, Full-Stack, and AI. Inspect code defense videos, audit commit trails, and 1-click export talent directly."
   - **Persona 3: The Tech Leader / Organizer**
     - Value Prop: "Host high-stakes corporate hackathons and hiring challenges. Automated rubric judging, conflict-of-interest triggers, live scoreboards, and seamless prize distribution."
